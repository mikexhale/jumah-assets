Before you write

The app did not follow the imam

Check that "In the mosque" is selected in More → Settings → Listening in prayer and that the mosque listener is downloaded (More → Settings → Downloads). Sit where the speakers are clearly audible and keep the phone out of a pocket. Very quiet or heavily echoing rooms take longer to lock on; the first translation usually arrives within the first verse after Al-Fatiha.

Praying at home, it hears nothing

Choose "Praying alone" in More → Settings → Listening in prayer. That mode listens to your own voice and works without the download.

No sound in my earphones

Prayer mode plays through connected earphones only. Confirm they are connected in Control Center and that the phone is not on silent for calls. More → Settings → Language shows the language the voice uses.

The translation is in the wrong language

More → Settings → Language sets the language for prayer, Study and History. Every translation language has a natural spoken voice: download it once in More → Settings → Downloads, or it streams while you are online. Only when a verse has not been downloaded and you are offline does the voice built into iOS read it.

How much space does the app use?

The app itself is small: it ships with the verses prayed most often. The mosque listener (about 450 MB) is downloaded once. The full offline Quran for your language, the spoken translation and the Arabic recitation (about 1.6 GB for English), is optional and can be removed in More → Settings → Downloads.

Subscriptions and refunds

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: open Settings on your iPhone → your name → Subscriptions. Refunds are requested at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Report a recognition problem

Tell us the mosque or recording, the surah, and roughly when it went wrong. If you are comfortable, turn on More → Settings → Help improve Jumu'ah → Share diagnostics with the developer for one prayer, then tap "Send today's log now"; it sends technical logs (never audio) that let us replay the session.

Privacy Policy

Jumu'ah for iOS · Effective 5 September 2026 · Developer: Thaer Ahmad

Jumu'ah listens to Quran recitation and plays the translation in your earphones. It is built to work on your iPhone without an account. This page explains exactly what leaves your device and why.

The short version

  • No account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics trackers, and no sale of data.
  • In the mosque, recitation is recognized on your iPhone. Praying alone, Recite and the short phrases the app listens for (Ameen, takbir, salaam) may use Apple's speech recognition, which can process audio on Apple's servers under Apple's privacy policy. Audio is never sent to our servers and is not recorded.
  • Your location is used on the device for prayer times and the Qibla direction and is never sent to us.
  • Three optional features contact our server: AI verse explanations, AI topic search and premium spoken translations. They send verse references and, for explanations and search, the words you typed. They never send audio, location, or your identity.

What the app processes and where

DataPurposeWhere it is processed
Microphone audioIdentifying the verse being recited and following the imamOn your iPhone (a speech model bundled with or downloaded by the app). Short phrases such as "Ameen" may be recognized by Apple's speech service under Apple's privacy policy.
LocationPrayer times, adhan reminders, Qibla directionOn your iPhone only.
Verse references (for example "2:255") and your languageFetching premium spoken translations, AI explanations and AI topic searchOur server (hosted on Modal). Explanations and search suggestions are generated with Google Gemini (explanations from classical tafsir text); the request contains the verse reference or search words, your language, and any question you typed. Responses are cached without any identifier so the same request is not generated twice.
Prayer historyLetting you replay what you heardStored on your iPhone. You can delete entries in the app.
Diagnostic logsFixing recognition and playback problemsOff by default. If you turn on "Share diagnostics with the developer" in Settings → Help improve Jumu'ah, the app's technical log (timings, recognized text and verse numbers, what was played, error messages, the phone model, iOS version and app build — never audio, your location or your name) is sent to our server and deleted within 30 days. You can turn it off at any time; "Send today's log now" sends only when you tap it.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple through the App Store. We receive no card details and no name; Apple gives the app only an anonymous receipt to confirm what you bought.

Children

Jumu'ah does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Your choices

  • Microphone and location permissions can be changed at any time in iOS Settings; the app keeps working with reduced features.
  • AI explanations and premium voices can be switched off in Settings; the app then uses the voices built into iOS.
  • Deleting the app removes all data stored on your iPhone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published here with a new effective date.

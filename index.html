Jumu'ah

Hear the meaning of every verse.

Jumu'ah listens to the recitation during prayer and plays the translation in your earphones, verse by verse, as the imam recites. It recognizes the Quran on your iPhone and works in the mosque or when you pray alone.

  • Prayer mode: follows the imam from the speakers and speaks each verse's meaning as it is being recited.
  • 36 languages, with premium spoken voices for the most common ones.
  • Recite: word-by-word feedback and memorization practice.
  • Study: read with translation and tafsir, and ask for an explanation of any verse.
  • Prayer times, adhan reminders, and Qibla.

Available on the App Store for iPhone (iOS 17 or later).
